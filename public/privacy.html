Expenses

Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Expenses is a personal money tracker: you import your bank statements, and it shows where your money goes. It is a private app used by its owner and the people they invite. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Where your data is kept

  • On your device. Transactions, categories, rules and budgets are stored in your browser's local storage on each device you use. They are not sent to the app's server.
  • In your own Google Drive, encrypted. If you turn on sync, the app saves one file (and weekly backup copies) in an Expenses folder in your Google Drive. The file is encrypted on your device with a passphrase that only you know (AES-256). Neither Google nor the app's owner can read it.

What the app gets from your Google account

  • Your email address, to check that your account is allowed to use the app and to show who is signed in.
  • Access to the files the app creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). The app cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

The app's server (Cloudflare Pages Functions) only handles sign-in. It keeps your Google sign-in in an encrypted, http-only cookie on your device, so the app can renew access without asking you to sign in every hour. The server does not store your data, your email address or your sign-in anywhere else.

Use of Google user data

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to sign you in and to read and write the app's own sync file. It is never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Hosting

The app is served by Cloudflare. Like any website host, Cloudflare processes technical request data (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Removing your data

  • Sign out in the app's Settings to end the sign-in on a device.
  • Remove the app's access at any time in your Google account: Google Account → Third-party connections.
  • Delete the Expenses folder in your Google Drive to remove the synced copy, and clear the site's data in your browser to remove the copy on a device.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the app's owner through the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
